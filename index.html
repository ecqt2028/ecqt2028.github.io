ECQT 2028

European Conference on Quantum Technologies

Bilbao, Spain

Welcome

The European Conference on Quantum Technologies (ECQT 2028) will take place in Bilbao, Spain, during the week beginning 10 July 2028.

Important: The exact conference dates will be announced at a later stage.

ECQT is a major forum for researchers, academics, industry representatives, and students working in all areas of quantum technologies, including quantum computing, quantum communication, quantum sensing, quantum information science, and related fields.

Location

ECQT 2028 will be hosted in the vibrant city of Bilbao, located in the Basque Country of northern Spain. The city is known for its modern architecture, rich culture, excellent gastronomy, and welcoming atmosphere.
