Venue


The conference will be held in Bizkaia Aretoa, which is in the center of Bilbao. Bizkaia Aretoa is the University of the Basque Country's most emblematic building for organizing social, cultural, academic, and scientific events, whether organized by the university itself or external entities.

The exact address is: Avda. Abandoibarra, 3, Bilbao.

Coming to Bilbao


The easiest way to reach Bilbao is by plane. Bilbao Airport is well connected to Europe's major airports (Frankfurt, Paris, Brussels, London Gatwick, Rome, Oslo, Zurich, Vienna...). Regular buses run from Bilbao Airport to the city center; the trip takes about 20 minutes.
